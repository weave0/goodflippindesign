# SKY MONEY Roadmap - June 2026

## Project Definition

**SKY MONEY** is the operating roadmap for turning the full GFV/GFD constellation into a high-traffic, high-trust, multi-site revenue ecosystem.

This is not a web design agency plan. SKY MONEY is a coordinated adoption, publishing, community, sponsorship, supporter, and premium knowledge strategy across public-interest technology, civic tools, AI education, cultural discovery, creative media, music, and local/regional storytelling.

## North Star

Build a network of useful public sites that people can discover, trust, share, support, sponsor, and return to.

Money comes from the sky only after the network has lift: traffic, clarity, trust, repeat use, sponsor-safe inventory, supporter identity, and premium value.

## Strategic Pillars

### 1. Cohesion Before Scale

Every public property needs to feel like part of one intentional ecosystem while preserving its own purpose.

Deliverables:

- shared ecosystem footer,
- shared `Support the ecosystem` CTA,
- `Brought to you by Good Flippin Design` or studio-credit block where appropriate,
- JSON-LD `@graph` that connects the properties,
- consistent newsletter/support capture pattern,
- cross-site links that help users move by interest: AI, civic, culture, data, music, community.

### 2. Adoption Before Monetization Widgets

Monetization follows clarity and usefulness.

Deliverables:

- first-screen positioning pass on each site,
- mobile UX and accessibility pass on top entry pages,
- top-10 page improvement list based on traffic potential,
- content hub pages by theme,
- no cluttered ad inventory on trust-sensitive pages.

### 3. Supporter Identity

Support should feel like joining the work, not tipping a random page.

Deliverables:

- monthly patron ladder,
- public use-of-funds block,
- supporter updates,
- project-specific funding goals,
- shared supporter language across GFD, GFV, AIAIMate, CitizenApproved, CultureSherpa, GlobalDeets, Lowertown, HeavyMoose, Red Leopard, Good Flippin News, and Good Flippin Luck.

### 4. Sponsor-Safe Inventory

The sites need packaged inventory that ethical sponsors can understand.

Inventory categories:

- AI literacy guides,
- civic explainers and public tools,
- cultural atlas modules,
- data briefs and dashboards,
- local Lowertown briefings,
- music/canon artist pages,
- public status and transparency reports.

### 5. Premium Knowledge and Creative Products

Keep core resources public, then sell deeper or more convenient formats.

Products:

- AI literacy starter kit,
- civic action toolkit,
- GlobalDeets dashboard/report templates,
- CultureSherpa learning packs,
- Lowertown quarterly development brief,
- music supporter bundles,
- Red Leopard / Heavy Moose digital press and art packs,
- ecosystem supporter bundle.

### 6. Institutional and Grant Pathways

The ecosystem has grant-aligned surfaces. SKY MONEY should prepare those doors without waiting on them.

Targets:

- AI literacy and workforce development,
- civic participation,
- digital inclusion,
- cultural preservation,
- local arts/culture,
- public-interest data and transparency,
- mediation/conflict-resolution and civic peace.

## Roadmap Phases

### Phase 0: Naming and Control Plane - Days 0-3

Goal: make SKY MONEY real as an internal project with one canonical roadmap.

Actions:

- Keep [SKY_MONEY_ROADMAP_2026-06.md](SKY_MONEY_ROADMAP_2026-06.md) as the source of truth.
- Link it from [REVENUE_ACTION_PLAN_2026-06.md](REVENUE_ACTION_PLAN_2026-06.md) or fold both later.
- Create a one-page public-safe ecosystem overview.
- Define the shared CTA language: `Support the ecosystem`, `Sponsor this work`, `Partner with us`.

Exit criteria:

- one canonical roadmap,
- one sponsor/supporter thesis,
- one support CTA phrase,
- one use-of-funds message.

## 12-Month Milestones

### 30 Days

- SKY MONEY roadmap exists and is canonical.
- Unified ecosystem support CTA is live or staged.
- Sponsor/supporter one-sheet is drafted.
- Donation trust language is corrected or verified.
- Top 20 pages are ranked by adoption potential.

### 60 Days

- Cross-site analytics events are live on core sites.
- One premium product is live.
- One sponsor-safe brief is live.
- Lowertown, HeavyMoose, Red Leopard, or BrettLeeWeaver has a cleaner support path.
- First sponsor/funder outreach wave sent.

### 90 Days

- 25-100 monthly patrons.
- 1-2 sponsor conversations active.
- One commissioned intelligence or sponsored brief closed or in proposal.
- One grant/institutional packet ready.
- Public ecosystem overview page live.

### 6 Months

- Multi-site traffic loop is measurable.
- At least three premium/support products exist.
- Sponsor-safe inventory exists across AI, civic, culture/data, local, and music.
- Weekly/monthly supporter updates are sustainable.
- Internal media platform reduces manual publishing friction.

### 12 Months

- Recurring supporter/sponsor base exists.
- The ecosystem is recognized as a coherent public-interest technology and creative network.
- Revenue comes from multiple aligned streams: patrons, sponsors, premium products, grants, selective commissioned intelligence, ads/affiliates, and music/creative support.

## What Not To Do

- Do not let commodity web design become the identity.
- Do not add ads to trust-sensitive civic or donation pages just because they are easy.
- Do not launch new sites without ecosystem footer, analytics, support path, and structured data.
- Do not let internal tooling replace public shipping.
- Do not build another CMS before using the admin/media tooling already present.
- Do not chase every domain equally; rank by adoption, leverage, and maintenance cost.

## Current Best Bet

SKY MONEY should spend the next 30 days making the ecosystem legible, measurable, supportable, and sponsor-ready. The fastest durable money is not one-off design work. It is turning existing sites into a network people understand, trust, revisit, and want to keep alive.
