Editorial integrity

What sponsorship is not

Sponsor-safe boundaries are enforced to protect trust. Sponsors fund the work — they don't control conclusions.

No deceptive endorsements Sponsorship is always clearly disclosed and labeled. No native-ad deception.
No political persuasion Civic tools are built for clarity and access — not to advocate for a party or candidate.
No crisis-page ads Donation pages, legal pages, mental health resources, and civic trust pages stay ad-free.
Editorial independence Sponsors do not influence research conclusions, data presentation, or public guidance.
Brand fit required Every sponsor relationship goes through an editorial-fit review. Misaligned sponsors are declined.
Full disclosure All sponsorship is disclosed to readers on the relevant pages, consistent with publisher policy.
