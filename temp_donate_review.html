The Founder's Story

Having spent 30+ years humbly exploring and researching complex subjects through interpersonal connection, digital exploration, and countless rabbit holes, I've found myself uniquely equipped to share this work openly.

CultureSherpa, CitizenApproved, AI Aimate, and Good Flippin Vibes are all personally funded projects — and I'll humbly avoid confessing how much I've contributed financially and emotionally. These are Net Loss projects I intend to keep alive until I'm no longer able to sustain them... because they have enough merit of substance and purpose to warrant such valiant attempts!

As someone with neurodiversity who hyperfocuses on complex models and systems, I may not be out and about much — but I have "one of those stories that most folks pull up a chair for, and feel inspired by in the end." Your support keeps these resources free and accessible for everyone who needs them.

— Brett Weaver, Founder & Creator
