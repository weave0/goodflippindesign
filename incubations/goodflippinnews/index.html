How Good Flippin News Came To Life

  1. Act I: Shariff finds an old printing press and asks, "What if headlines healed?"
  2. Act II: DJ Z wires speakers into the press so each story leaves with a soundtrack.
  3. Act III: Heavy Moose designs the layout where every article includes a way to help.
  4. Act IV: The alley cats form the verification desk: fact-check first, sparkle second.
  5. Act V: Goodflippinnews.com goes live at dawn with one promise: no doomscrolling required.
