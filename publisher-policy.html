Publisher and Monetization Policy

Effective: June 10, 2026 · Last updated: June 10, 2026
Plain-language note: This page defines our editorial standards, monetization safeguards, and sensitive-content boundaries for ads shown on goodflippindesign.com.

1) Scope and ownership

This policy applies to content published on goodflippindesign.com, operated by GFV LLC DBA Good Flippin Design. Our consumer-facing brand site is goodflippinvibes.com.

2) Original-content standards

  • We publish original project documentation, implementation detail, and platform-specific case studies.
  • We avoid thin pages, duplicate placeholders, and auto-generated filler content.
  • Each published page must provide clear user value, practical guidance, or verifiable portfolio evidence.

3) Sensitive events and conflict-content policy

Due to the war in Ukraine and other ongoing humanitarian crises, we do not monetize content that exploits, dismisses, or condones real-world violence, warfare, or human suffering.

  • We do not publish sensational, celebratory, or dehumanizing treatment of violent events.
  • We remove or de-monetize content that risks harmful framing of conflict and civilian harm.
  • When sensitive events are discussed, the purpose must be informational, respectful, and non-exploitative.

4) Ad and affiliate transparency

  • Sponsored placements are clearly labeled.
  • Advertising does not alter our editorial conclusions.
  • We do not use deceptive ad placement, cloaking, or hidden monetization mechanics.

5) AI-assisted content safeguards

  • AI-assisted drafts are reviewed and edited by a human before publication.
  • We fact-check technical claims and remove unsupported assertions.
  • We do not publish fabricated credentials, fabricated client outcomes, or synthetic testimonials.

6) Quality control and corrections

  • We periodically update pages with stale technical guidance.
  • Material corrections are made promptly when errors are identified.
  • Pages that no longer meet quality standards are revised, redirected, or removed.

7) Reporting concerns

If you believe a page violates this policy, email us at getsome@goodflippinvibes.com with the URL and concern details.
