Privacy Policy

Effective: March 5, 2026 · Last updated: March 5, 2026
Plain-language note: This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the limited ways we share it. It’s not legal advice, and it should be reviewed by a qualified attorney for your specific situation.

1) Who we are

goodflippinvibes.com (the "Site") is operated by GFV LLC, doing business as Good Flippin Design ("GFD", "we", "us", "our").

2) Information we collect

We collect information in a few ways:

  • Information you provide: when you submit a contact or inquiry form, request an NDA/SOW/agreement, or otherwise message us (e.g., your name, email, company, message content).
  • Donations/payments: if you donate, payment is processed by Stripe. We do not store full payment card numbers on our servers.
  • Community/admin access: if you sign in to community or admin areas, authentication may be handled by Clerk. Clerk may collect and process account identifiers (e.g., email) and session information.
  • Usage data: we use Google Analytics to understand traffic and basic usage patterns (for example, pages visited, approximate location, device/browser information, and interaction events).
  • Operational logs: our hosting and security providers (including Cloudflare) may process IP address, request metadata, and similar technical data to deliver and protect the Site.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information (e.g., Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, medical information) through general web forms.

3) Cookies and analytics

We and our analytics providers may use cookies and similar technologies to measure Site performance and improve experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings and may be able to opt out of certain analytics using provider tools.

Google Analytics is provided by Google. Google’s use of data is governed by Google’s policies.

4) How we use information

We use information to:

  • Respond to messages, inquiries, and requests.
  • Provide and improve the Site and our services.
  • Process donations and maintain records related to transactions.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and debug issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

5) How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: with vendors that help us operate the Site (for example, Cloudflare for hosting/security, Formspree for forms, Stripe for payments, Clerk for authentication, Google Analytics for analytics).
  • Legal and safety: if required by law, subpoena, or to protect rights, safety, and security.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (subject to applicable law).

6) Data retention

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

7) Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can’t guarantee absolute security.

8) Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete certain personal information. You can request help by emailing us.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings and may opt out of certain analytics as offered by the provider.

9) Children’s privacy

The Site is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10) Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date controls. Your continued use of the Site after updates means you accept the updated policy.
