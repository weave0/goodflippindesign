## Confirmed Facts

### Downtown Saint Paul — By the Numbers

_(Source: Saint Paul Downtown Alliance, downtownstpaul.com)_

- **10,572** current downtown residents
- **$4.2 billion** total downtown property value
- **54,000** workers in downtown Saint Paul
- **#2** park system in the country (per national rankings cited by Downtown Alliance)

### Riverfront Investment

_(Source: saintpaulriverfront.com, stpaul.gov)_

- **$1 billion+** in public and private investments currently planned for the Saint Paul riverfront
- **Xcel Energy Center Arena Complex renovation** — $488M project, would modernize the arena for the Minnesota Wild; pending state bonding ($50M request)
- Multiple additional projects (see saintpaulriverfront.com for interactive map — 12+ projects listed)

### Downtown Revitalization Strategy

_(Source: stpaul.gov/departments/planning-and-economic-development/planning/revitalizing-downtown)_

- City officially framing current moment as "once-in-a-generation opportunity"
- Three pillars: (1) Safety investment, (2) Green spaces, (3) Office-to-housing conversion
- City goal: attract **20,000 new downtown residents**

### Office-to-Housing Conversions

_(Source: stpaul.gov)_

- **Landmark Tower** → 175+ apartments (opened Spring 2025)
- **The Stella** (former Ecolab HQ) → 178 apartments (in progress)
- **10 additional** downtown buildings identified by outside study as strong conversion candidates
- Study commissioned by Saint Paul Downtown Alliance

### Parks & Public Spaces

_(Source: stpaul.gov)_

- **Pedro Park** — opened September 2025 at 10th & Robert Streets; event pavilion, play area, dog run, gardens; result of 10-year planning effort
- **Ecolab beautification** — $2M+ investment in new pedestrian space outside Ecolab HQ
- **Capital City Bikeway** — protected cycling routes throughout downtown, ongoing expansion

### Economic Development Tools

_(Source: stpaul.gov)_

- **Opportunity Zone: City Center and Riverfront** — federally designated, capital gains tax advantages for qualifying investments
- **Downtown Vitality Fund** — city economic development fund for downtown businesses and projects
- **Cultural STAR Grant Program** — city arts/culture funding; capital projects, capacity building, special projects
- **Neighborhood STAR Grant Program** — neighborhood business grants
- **Business Assistance Fund** — city business support
- **Emerging and BIPOC Developer Training** — city program for new developers
- Heritage Preservation Commission — supports historic districts including Lowertown

## Known Facts (Well-Documented, Source Not Scraped)

### History & National Historic District

- Lowertown is a **federally designated National Historic District** (NPS Ref# 83001357, designated ~1983)
- Established circa **1850s** as the commercial freight hub of the upper Midwest
- Dense network of brick warehouse buildings, rail yards, and freight depots along the Mississippi
- Roughly bounded by: Mississippi River (south), I-94 (north), Robert Street (west), Broadway Street (east)
- Began transitioning to arts use in the **1970s–1980s** as industrial uses declined

### Artspace & the Artist Loft Movement

- **Artspace Projects** (artspaceprojects.org) pioneered live-work artist housing in Lowertown
- **Lowertown Lofts** — converted 19th-century warehouse into artist live-work spaces; launched ca. late 1980s/early 1990s
- Became a **national model** for arts-driven urban revitalization, replicated in cities worldwide
- Made Lowertown one of America's earliest sustained artist colonies

### Key Landmarks

- **Mears Park** — central green space and outdoor performance venue; the "living room" of Lowertown; hosts free summer concerts (Lowertown Live series), festivals, community events
- **Union Depot** (1881 original, rebuilt 1926) — Beaux-Arts masterpiece by Charles A. Reed; one of the most beautifully restored train stations in America; now a multi-modal transit hub + events venue; Green Line terminus
- **CHS Field** — home of the **St. Paul Saints** (AAA affiliate); opened **2015** in heart of Lowertown; known for its intimate atmosphere and creative fan experience; MiLB stadium architecture award winner
- **Lowertown Farmers Market** — one of Minnesota's oldest and largest open-air markets; operates weekends May–October at Fifth & Broadway; produce, local makers, community institution
- **Black Dog Coffee & Wine Bar** — iconic neighborhood venue; art, jazz, live music; a cultural anchor; located in a converted Lowertown warehouse

### Restaurants & Food

- **Mickey's Diner** — 1939 Art Deco lunch car (Paramount design); open 24/7; a national landmark; listed on National Register of Historic Places
- **Meritage** — acclaimed French brasserie on St. Peter Street; award-winning wine list; a fine dining anchor
- **Cossetta Alimentari** — legendary Italian deli, bakery, and restaurant; a Saint Paul institution
- **The Lowertown Tap Room** — craft beer culture
- Diverse food scene: Vietnamese, Somali, Ethiopian, Mexican alongside New American fine dining

### Transit & Connectivity

- **Green Line Light Rail (METRO Green)** — runs Minneapolis–Saint Paul; multiple stops serve Lowertown/downtown (Union Depot station, 4th & Cedar); direct connection to MSP Airport via Blue Line transfer at Downtown East
- **Metro Transit buses** — extensive network
- **Amtrak** — Empire Builder train stops at Union Depot (Chicago–Seattle/Portland route)
- **I-94** — major interstate access from Minneapolis/Twin Cities metro
- **MSP Airport** — approximately 20 minutes by rail

### Architecture (Notable Buildings)

- **Hamm Building** — 14-story commercial building; Romanesque/Gothic Revival; built 1920; on Fourth Street
- **Pioneer Building** (also: Pioneer Press Building) — 1889; Romanesque Revival
- **First National Bank Building** — Modernist skyscraper
- **Lowertown artist warehouse lofts** — converted 19th-century warehouses with exposed brick, timber frames, soaring ceilings
- **Minnesota Boat Club** boathouse (1890) — along the river

### Culture & Events

- **Lowertown Live** — free outdoor summer music series in Mears Park
- **Art galleries open late** on First Thursday of each month
- **Saint Paul Winter Carnival** (based in downtown Saint Paul) — world's largest winter festival; dates back to 1886
- **Flint Hills Family Fest** (referenced on stpaul.gov) — family festival
- Lowertown has a strong jazz/blues/indie music history; multiple small venues

5. **10,572 current residents + 54K workers** — active daily economic base
6. **$4.2B property value** — established asset class, not speculative
7. **Cultural STAR grants + Downtown Vitality Fund** — de-risk arts/culture investments with city subsidies

9. **Arena complex $488M upgrade** pending — massive anchor for the entertainment district
