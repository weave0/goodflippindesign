# SKY MONEY Outreach Starter

## Purpose

This starter kit turns the SKY MONEY plan into messages Brett can send today. The goal is not to explain the whole universe. The goal is to open aligned conversations around support, sponsorship, partnerships, and commissioned knowledge work.

Primary links:

- Ecosystem overview: <https://goodflippindesign.com/ecosystem-overview.html>
- Support: <https://goodflippindesign.com/donate.html>
- Sponsor/partner inquiry: <https://goodflippindesign.com/investors.html>
- Publisher policy: <https://goodflippindesign.com/publisher-policy.html>

## Sponsor-Fit Criteria

Good first-wave leads should match at least three of these:

- They fund or benefit from AI education, civic trust, culture, data, local media, music, or public-interest technology.
- They already sponsor local events, newsletters, education programs, arts/culture work, or civic/community resources.
- They need credible digital storytelling, dashboards, explainers, guides, or audience trust.
- They can make a small decision without months of procurement.
- They know Brett or can be reached through a warm introduction.
- Their brand would not create trust problems on civic, legal, donation, or public resource pages.
- They can start with `$250-$1,500/month`, a one-time sponsored brief, in-kind support, or a partner introduction.

## Five Warm Message Templates

### 1. Individual Supporter

Subject: Quick ask for the Good Flippin ecosystem

Hi [Name],

I’m tightening the public support path for Good Flippin Design / Good Flippin Vibes: AI education, civic tools, culture/data work, music, and local media surfaces that I’ve been building mostly out of pocket.

The simplest support path is here: <https://goodflippindesign.com/donate.html>

Even `$5-$25/month` helps keep the public work moving while I package the sponsor and partner side more clearly. No pressure at all, but if the work feels aligned, your support or a warm intro would mean a lot.

- Brett

### 2. Sponsor Lead

Subject: Possible sponsor fit: [AI education / civic tools / culture-local media]

Hi [Name],

I’m opening a small first wave of sponsor conversations for the Good Flippin ecosystem. The public overview is here:

<https://goodflippindesign.com/ecosystem-overview.html>

The cleanest sponsor lanes are AI Education, Civic/Data Tools, and Culture/Creative/Local Media. I’m looking for aligned partners who want to help fund useful public resources without turning them into cluttered ad pages.

Would a short conversation make sense next week to see whether one lane fits [Organization]?

- Brett

### 3. Partner / Institution

Subject: Exploring an aligned public-resource partnership

Hi [Name],

I’m building the Good Flippin ecosystem into a coordinated public-resource network: AI education, civic guidance, cultural learning, data storytelling, local media, and creative work.

Overview: <https://goodflippindesign.com/ecosystem-overview.html>

I thought of [Organization] because [specific reason]. I’m interested in practical partnerships: one guide, one learning module, one civic/data resource, or one sponsor-supported public brief that creates visible value quickly.

Would you be open to a 20-minute conversation?

- Brett

### 4. Commissioned Intelligence Brief

Subject: A small commissioned brief idea for [Organization]

Hi [Name],

One of the clearest paid paths I’m packaging is a commissioned intelligence brief: a concise public or private explainer, dashboard, resource guide, or data story around a topic your audience already cares about.

The broader ecosystem overview is here: <https://goodflippindesign.com/ecosystem-overview.html>

A first brief can be scoped tightly: one topic, one audience, one useful output, usually in the `$1,500-$5,000` range depending on depth. I had an idea around [specific topic]. Worth a quick call?

- Brett

### 5. Warm Intro Ask

Subject: Do you know the right person for this?

Hi [Name],

I’m starting sponsor and partner outreach for the Good Flippin ecosystem: AI education, civic/data tools, culture/local media, and creative public-interest work.

Overview: <https://goodflippindesign.com/ecosystem-overview.html>

Do you know anyone at [category/org type] who supports useful public resources, local culture, civic trust, education, or data storytelling? A warm intro to one thoughtful person would help more than a broad blast.

- Brett
