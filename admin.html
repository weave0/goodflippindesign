🎯 Getting Started

This platform is live and ready. Here's the fastest path to your first scheduled CultureSherpa campaign:

  1. 1
    Upload or import your assets

    Go to → drag files in, click “Import Folder” for a whole directory, or click to scan your live sites and claim images already deployed.

  2. 2
    Create a campaign

    Go to → click New Campaign → name it “Culture Profile Drip” for CultureSherpa.

  3. 3
    Connect your social platforms

    Go to → click Connect Account → use Manual Token (fastest) or complete OAuth setup for LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, and X.

  4. 4
    Build your 100-day drip

    Go to → select your campaign, set start date, pick 2 posts/day, all 4 platforms → import 200 content entries as JSON → Preview → Confirm. Your entire year-quarter is scheduled in one click.
