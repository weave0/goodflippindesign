### **Step 2: Initial Review**

- Download generated image
- Test at various sizes (resize to 512px, 128px, 32px, 16px)
- View on dark background (#0d0d0d)
- Check circular crop (use online tool or Photoshop mask)

### **Step 3: Decision Point**

✅ **If Perfect (90%+ match):** Proceed to vectorization
⚠️ **If Close (70-89%):** Note specific issues, generate ONE refined version
❌ **If Misaligned (<70%):** Revise prompt (identify root cause: style? concept? complexity?)

### **Step 4: Vectorization**

- Import into Adobe Illustrator or Figma
- Trace carefully (preserve glow effects as separate layers)
- Separate layers: background, main symbol, glow/accent, effects
- Save as SVG (vector) + export high-res PNG (4096x4096 transparent)

### **Step 5: Color Variants**

From the master vector, create:

1. **Full Color** (primary - dark bg + accent glow)
2. **Monochrome Light** (white/light gray on dark - for dark mode sites)
3. **Monochrome Dark** (black/dark gray on light - for light bg documents)
4. **Flat Version** (no glow, solid colors - for print/small sizes)
