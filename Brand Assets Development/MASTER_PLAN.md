## 🎨 The Logo We're Creating

**Brand Essence:**

- **Personality:** Friendly Expert (warm humanity + technical excellence)
- **Visual Style:** Minimalist with warmth, glowing geometric precision
- **Color Palette:** Dark theme (#0d0d0d) + ONE vibrant accent glow
- **Symbolism:** Global intelligence, accessible technology, human-centered innovation

**Concept Options (AI will choose strongest):**

1. **Luminous Lettermark** - Stylized "GFD" with integrated glow/data flow
2. **Global Intelligence Node** - Interconnected nodes forming circular globe pattern
3. **Human-Tech Fusion** - Organic + geometric elements unified by warm glow

**Technical Requirements:**

- Scalable 16px to infinity (favicon to billboard)
- Circular crop safe (Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn profiles)
- Works in monochrome (print, accessibility)
- Vector-traceable (clean geometric paths)
- Social-first optimized

**Success Criteria:**
✅ Instantly recognizable
✅ Warm + Professional balance
✅ Scalable without quality loss
✅ Accessible (high contrast, WCAG AA)
✅ Timeless design (5+ year shelf life)
✅ Globally inclusive (no cultural bias)
✅ Ecosystem aligned (fits with CultureSherpa, AI Aimate, GFV)
