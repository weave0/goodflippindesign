# Good Flippin Ecosystem Sponsor One-Sheet

## Public Positioning

Good Flippin Design is the studio and infrastructure hub behind a public-interest network of education, civic, culture, data, local media, and creative projects. Good Flippin Vibes is the audience-facing creative/community brand. Together, the ecosystem turns complex subjects into usable public resources people can learn from, share, support, and return to.

This is not a generic ad buy. Sponsorship supports durable public tools and sponsor-safe publishing lanes while preserving editorial trust.

## What Sponsors Fund

Sponsors help cover hosting, maintenance, accessibility improvements, research, writing, testing, design, public documentation, outreach, and the production time required to keep the network moving.

Support keeps core resources free or low-friction while enabling better guides, briefings, dashboards, learning modules, civic tools, and creative releases.

## Buyable Sponsor Lanes

### 1. AI Education

Best fit: workforce development groups, schools, libraries, AI tooling companies, training providers, local employers, digital inclusion partners.

What it funds:

- Practical AI literacy guides and learning tracks.
- Public explainers that make AI safer and easier to understand.
- Searchable knowledge resources, starter kits, and community learning paths.
- Accessibility and plain-language editing for nontechnical audiences.

Example sponsor language:

> Supported by partners who believe practical AI education should be accessible beyond expensive courses and closed networks.

Starter package: `$250-$1,500/month` depending on placement, duration, and deliverables.

### 2. Civic/Data Tools

Best fit: civic groups, public-interest organizations, legal-adjacent service providers, data teams, local institutions, foundations, mediation/conflict-resolution partners.

What it funds:

- CitizenApproved-style civic guidance and resource pages.
- MinnesotaPeace-style trust, mediation, and civic peace surfaces.
- Data briefs, dashboards, transparency explainers, and public resource guides.
- Plain-language improvements for confusing public processes.

Example sponsor language:

> Supported by partners who want clearer civic information, stronger public trust, and more usable data in the hands of communities.

Starter package: `$250-$1,500/month`; sponsored briefs or dashboards typically start at `$1,500-$5,000`.

### 3. Culture / Creative / Local Media

Best fit: local businesses, arts organizations, venues, cultural institutions, music supporters, tourism/community groups, neighborhood partners.

What it funds:

- Cultural discovery pages, local briefings, and creative publishing.
- Good Flippin Vibes, Heavy Moose, and related music/art surfaces.
- Lowertown and regional storytelling resources.
- Sponsor-safe creative campaigns and transparent supporter updates.

Example sponsor language:

> Supported by partners who believe local culture, creative work, and community storytelling deserve resilient digital infrastructure.

Starter package: `$250-$1,500/month`; custom creative or local-media packages priced by scope.

## Sponsor-Safety Boundaries

Good Flippin Design does not sell deceptive endorsements, political persuasion inventory, crisis-page ads, or sponsorship that would compromise trust-sensitive civic, legal, donation, or public resource pages.

Sponsor relationships should be clearly disclosed, contextually relevant, and aligned with the public purpose of the funded lane.
