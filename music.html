Meet Heavy Moose

A massive human-formed moose in dark tailored layers, antler silhouette just visible above the coat collar. He represents the darker and painful side of healing — with levity, because he's a heavy moose who sometimes plays banjo.

The DROSS albums are his work — industrial soundscapes built from grief, iron, and the kind of patience that only comes after you've stopped running.
