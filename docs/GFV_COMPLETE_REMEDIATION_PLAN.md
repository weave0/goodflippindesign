- [ ] 100% of health claims have direct PubMed/NIH citations
- [ ] 100% of science pages have evidence level badges
- [ ] 100% of science pages have limitations sections
- [ ] 100% of science pages have author attribution
- [ ] Effect sizes clearly communicated (not just p-values)

- [ ] Scientific Advisory Board page live with verified credentials

- [ ] Correction policy in place

- [ ] Clear content type labels (Science vs Practice vs Community)
- [ ] Intuitive navigation (tested with users)
- [ ] Lighthouse score: Performance 90+, Accessibility 95+

- [ ] Page load time <3 seconds (median)
