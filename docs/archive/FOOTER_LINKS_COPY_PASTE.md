## 🎯 Pro Tips

1. **Match Your Theme:** Adjust colors in `style="color: #8b5cf6"` to match site
2. **Custom Message:** Edit the `<p>` tag to emphasize your site's unique mission
3. **WordPress:** Use a Custom HTML widget in footer
4. **React/Next.js:** Convert to component with your styling
5. **Existing Footer:** Add within current footer structure (don't duplicate footer)
