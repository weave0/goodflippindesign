# Content Guidelines — Messaging Tact

_Last updated: 2026-02-11_

## Core Principle

**Be agnostic when talking about experience.** Show tact and character by being ambiguous about names of actual entities (employers, clients, partners), while still conveying the scope and impact of the work.

## Evidence from Directive Transcript

> **Timestamp 00:06:10–00:06:25**:
> "I'd rather you be ambiguous about names of actual entities. You know, even Piedmont Global to an extent. I think it shows a lot of tact and character and just good graces. Be agnostic when talking about experience."

## What to Avoid

❌ **Don't mention**:

- Former employer names (e.g., "Piedmont Global", "Eliassen Group")
- Specific client organizations
- Partner company names
- Healthcare system names (even if work was done for them)

## What IS Okay

✅ **DO mention**:

- Your **own** projects and platforms (AI Aimate, CultureSherpa, Good Flippin Vibes, etc.)
- Generic descriptors of experience:
  - "Major healthcare organization"
  - "National healthcare network"
  - "Enterprise clients"
  - "Fortune 500 companies"
  - "Healthcare leadership teams"
  - "Cross-country sales presentations"
  - "Multi-million dollar opportunities"
- Capabilities and technical stack
- Industry sectors (healthcare, compliance, education)
- Outcomes and impact

## Copy Review Checklist

Before publishing new content, check:

- [ ] No former employer names mentioned
- [ ] No client organization names mentioned
- [ ] Experience described in generic but impressive terms
- [ ] Own projects can be named (but domain mentions kept minimal in body copy)
- [ ] Technical capabilities and outcomes emphasized over entity names
- [ ] Industry context provided without specific organization attribution

## Examples of Good Messaging

✅ **Good**: "Presented to healthcare leadership teams for multi-million dollar opportunities"
❌ **Avoid**: "Presented to Kaiser Permanente for a $5M contract"

✅ **Good**: "Wrote the entire sales playbook for a major healthcare organization"
❌ **Avoid**: "Wrote the entire sales playbook for Piedmont Global"

✅ **Good**: "Enterprise market intelligence platform" (portfolio title)
✅ **Also Good**: "eliassen.globaldeets.com" (subdomain URL is fine; it's a technical identifier, not prominent copy)

✅ **Good**: "AI Aimate — AI Education Platform" (own project)
✅ **Good**: "CultureSherpa — Interactive Cultural Atlas" (own project)

## Rationale

This approach:

1. **Shows professional discretion** — doesn't name-drop clients/employers
2. **Emphasizes impact** — focuses on what was built and achieved
3. **Maintains credibility** — still provides context about scale and complexity
4. **Protects relationships** — avoids potential confidentiality issues
5. **Centers your work** — highlights your capabilities, not borrowed prestige from big names
