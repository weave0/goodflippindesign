## Role

Good Flippin Luck is a playful campaign, promo, and creative commerce surface. The current Maze Snake Fortune prototype can become a shareable hook for supporters, drops, music/art promotion, and light lead capture.

## Audience

Supporters, fans, casual visitors, music/art followers, and people who respond to interactive experiences before they read a long explanation.

## Primary CTA

Play, share, and join the supporter list.

## Secondary CTA

Discover the broader GFV creative ecosystem.

## First MVP

Keep the playable fortune game, then add an ecosystem footer, support/list CTA, clearer share path, and a launch checklist before expanding mechanics.

## Guardrails

- Do not build a large game platform before the offer is proven.
- Keep the first win/share/support path fast on mobile.
- Tie future drops or merch to measurable supporter action.

## Next Action

Add ecosystem footer, support/list CTA, canonical/meta pass, and verify the game on mobile before public launch.
