# Good Flippin News

Domain: goodflippinnews.com
Stage: seed
Registry slug: goodflippinnews
Workspace path: incubations/goodflippinnews

## Role

Good Flippin News is the ecosystem publishing and dispatch surface. It should turn useful updates, civic/creative progress, and sponsor-safe stories into a readable channel without becoming a generic news blog.

## Audience

Supporters, partners, local/civic/creative readers, and people who discover the ecosystem through stories rather than product pages.

## Primary CTA

Subscribe or follow ecosystem dispatches.

## Secondary CTA

Explore Good Flippin Design and Good Flippin Vibes.

## First MVP

One static dispatch page with a clear premise, three content lanes, an ecosystem footer, a subscribe/follow/support CTA, and a placeholder archive.

## Guardrails

- Do not turn it into doomscrolling or broad commodity news.
- Keep stories tied to useful action, ecosystem progress, local/civic value, or creative momentum.
- Avoid heavy CMS work until the dispatch cadence is proven.

## Next Action

Convert the incubation prototype into an MVP dispatch page with footer, canonical/meta pass, and measurable CTA.
