## 5. HOW WE PROTECT YOUR INFORMATION

### 5.1 Security Measures

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information:

**Administrative Safeguards:**

- Privacy and security training for personnel
- Access controls (need-to-know basis)
- Background checks for employees handling sensitive data
- Written information security policy

**Technical Safeguards:**

- Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS for Site)
- Encryption at rest (for stored client data)
- Secure password policies (hashed and salted)
- Multi-factor authentication (where feasible)
- Regular security updates and patches
- Firewall and intrusion detection

**Physical Safeguards:**

- Secure office access (if applicable)
- Locked storage for physical documents
- Device encryption for laptops and mobile devices
- Secure disposal of data (shredding, data wiping)

### 5.2 Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to:

- Fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy
- Comply with legal obligations (tax records: 7 years, contracts: statute of limitations)
- Resolve disputes and enforce agreements

**Retention Periods:**

- **Inquiry emails**: 2 years after last contact
- **Client project data**: 7 years after project completion (for legal/tax compliance)
- **Financial records**: 7 years (IRS requirement)
- **Marketing lists**: Until you opt out
- **Site analytics**: 26 months (Google Analytics default)

### 5.3 Limitations of Security

**No system is 100% secure.** While we use reasonable efforts to protect your information, we cannot guarantee:

- Prevention of all unauthorized access
- Data will never be disclosed due to breach
- Third-party services we use are breach-proof

**Your Responsibility:**

- Use strong, unique passwords
- Do not share login credentials
- Report suspected security issues promptly
- Keep your contact information current

### 5.4 Data Breach Response

In the event of a data breach:

1. **Immediate Actions** (within 24-48 hours):
   - Contain the breach and stop ongoing access
   - Assess scope of compromised data
   - Engage cybersecurity experts if needed

2. **Legal Compliance** (per MN Stat. § 325M):
   - Notify affected individuals without unreasonable delay
   - Notify MN Attorney General if 500+ Minnesota residents affected
   - Provide identity theft prevention resources (if applicable)

3. **Transparency**:
   - Post notice on Site if widespread breach
   - Offer credit monitoring services (if financial data compromised)
   - Communicate remediation steps taken
