# Performance scripts + cadence

Target vibe: deep baritone, Central African English accent _inspired cadence_ (clear open vowels, syllable-timed rhythm, strong consonants, controlled pacing).

Base line:

> "X is the set… of all things."

## 1) Whispered — Philosophical / Intimate

**Tone:** controlled breath, close-mic, intentional pauses.

**Script:**

> X… is the set…
> (pause)
> of all things.

**Cadence:**

- “X” — soft but grounded
- Slight downward tone on “set”
- Gentle falling cadence on “things”

## 2) Measured Authority — Academic / Mathematical Gravitas

**Tone:** calm, declarative, professor-level presence.

**Script:**

> X is the set.
> Of all things.

**Cadence:**

- Slight emphasis on **set**
- Slight pause before second phrase
- Strong consonants on _t_ in “set”

## 3) Rhythmic — Spoken Word / Poetic Pulse

**Tone:** subtle groove, slight stress pattern.

**Script:**

> X is the set —
> Of. All. Things.

**Cadence / timing:**

- Downbeat on “X”
- “Of. All. Things.” separated rhythmically
- Slight crescendo toward “things”

Beat guide (70–90 BPM feel):

> X (beat) is the SET (beat)
> Of (beat) ALL (beat) THINGS.

## 4) Powerful Proclamation — Epic / Cinematic

**Tone:** resonant chest voice, expansive.

**Script:**

> X…
> IS the set…
> of ALL things.

**Cadence:**

- Build intensity
- “ALL” emphasized
- Slight lift before final drop

## 5) Low-Baritone Chant (almost musical)

**Tone:** slow 70–80 BPM pulse.

**Script:**

> X is the set
> (beat)
> of all things
> (beat)

**Accent nuance:**

- “things” slightly softened final “gs”
- “set” clean and clipped
- Vowels open, not flattened

## If you want it to sound cinematic (post)

- Subtle room reverb (large hall, ~1.8s tail)
- Low pad underneath (D minor works well)
- Light sub-bass drone under proclamation version
- 60–80 BPM ambient pulse for rhythmic version
