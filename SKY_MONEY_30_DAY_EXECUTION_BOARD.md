# SKY MONEY 30-Day Execution Board

## Purpose

This board turns the broader SKY MONEY roadmap into the next 30 days of highest-impact work: stabilize the earning path, make the ecosystem easy to understand, and start conversations that can produce cash without turning the company into a commodity web shop.

## Current Assessment

The fastest path to earning is not a new build. The strongest near-term leverage is the production GFD/GFV stack because it already has traffic, donation rails, Stripe, PayPal.me, GoFundMe, investor intake, publisher policy, community/auth infrastructure, health monitoring, and admin tooling.

The stop/start risk is coming from scattered surfaces and too many partially monetizable ideas. The fix is to route attention through one funnel:

1. Public trust: explain the ecosystem clearly.
2. Support: make one-time and monthly support obvious.
3. Sponsor/partner: make aligned organizations understand what they can fund.
4. Premium/commissioned work: package one small paid product or intelligence offer.
5. Measurement: track which paths actually produce intent.

## Week 1: Make Support Trustworthy and Obvious

Goal: remove trust friction and make the public ask clear.

- Keep [SKY_MONEY_ROADMAP_2026-06.md](SKY_MONEY_ROADMAP_2026-06.md) as the strategic source of truth.
- Use this board as the active operating checklist.
- Remove or verify any tax-deductible donation language before active fundraising.
- Add a short public-safe ecosystem overview section or page that answers: what this is, why it matters, what support funds, and how sponsors/partners can help.
- Make [donate.html](donate.html) the main supporter path for one-time and monthly support.
- Make [investors.html](investors.html) the main strategic sponsor, investor, and partner path.
- Add consistent CTA language wherever possible: `Support the ecosystem`, `Sponsor this work`, `Partner with us`.

Exit criteria:

- Donation copy is legally cautious.
- Monthly patron path is visible.
- Sponsor/investor intake path is visible.
- The ecosystem can be understood in under 10 seconds from the main site.

## Week 2: Instrument the Earning Funnel

Goal: know what people click before expanding the system.

- Standardize event names across the core pages:
  - `support_cta_click`
  - `donation_start`
  - `patron_start`
  - `sponsor_inquiry_click`
  - `partner_inquiry_submit`
  - `ecosystem_outbound_click`
  - `premium_product_click`
- Confirm Cloudflare-injected ad and analytics env vars are configured where intended.
- Add UTM parameters to cross-site support links.
- Create a private weekly metrics note from GA4, Stripe, Formspree, and health/admin data.
- Rank the top 20 pages by likely conversion impact, not just narrative importance.

Exit criteria:

- Support clicks and sponsor-intent clicks are measurable.
- Cross-site referral flow is measurable.
- One simple weekly dashboard/report exists.

## Week 3: Package the First Things People Can Buy or Fund

Goal: stop selling the whole universe; sell three clear entry points.

Create these three packets:

| Packet                          |      Price / ask | Buyer                                                  | What it funds                                                  |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------: | ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ecosystem Patron                |      `$5-$50/mo` | individual supporters                                  | public tools, content, hosting, development time               |
| Category Sponsor                | `$250-$1,500/mo` | aligned orgs, local sponsors, education/civic partners | AI, civic, culture, data, music, or local resource lanes       |
| Commissioned Intelligence Brief |  `$1,500-$5,000` | businesses, nonprofits, civic/education orgs           | a useful data brief, explainer, dashboard, or resource package |

Minimum assets:

- One sponsor one-sheet.
- One use-of-funds block.
- One commissioned-intelligence offer page or PDF-ready section.
- One outreach list of 25 aligned people or orgs.

Exit criteria:

- A supporter knows how to help in 30 seconds.
- A sponsor knows what they are funding.
- A paid-work buyer sees a concrete offer instead of generic web design.

## Week 4: Launch Outreach and One Paid Product Experiment

Goal: begin earning attempts, not just readiness work.

- Send the first 25 sponsor/supporter/partner outreach messages.
- Publish one transparent supporter update.
- Stage or launch one small premium product:
  - AIAIMate AI literacy starter kit.
  - CitizenApproved civic action toolkit.
  - GlobalDeets data brief template.
  - CultureSherpa learning module.
  - GFV / Heavy Moose supporter bundle.
- Add the product to the support/sponsor narrative without hiding the free public mission.

Exit criteria:

- First outreach wave sent.
- One paid/premium experiment exists.
- At least one sponsor or commissioned-work conversation is opened.

## First 72 Hours

1. Finish donation trust cleanup.
2. Draft the public-safe ecosystem overview.
3. Draft sponsor one-sheet copy.
4. Add or verify support/sponsor CTAs on the highest-traffic GFD pages.
5. Build a 25-name outreach list.
6. Send 5 warm messages before polishing anything else.

## Guardrails

- Do not chase all domains equally.
- Do not put ads on trust-sensitive civic, legal, donation, or crisis-oriented pages.
- Do not lead with generic web design.
- Do not build new infrastructure before using the existing Stripe, Formspree, admin, health, and publisher-policy surface.
- Do not keep refining private docs if the public support and sponsor paths are not improving.

## Success Targets

30-day baseline:

- 10-25 monthly patrons or direct supporters.
- 25 outreach messages sent.
- 3-5 sponsor/partner conversations opened.
- 1 premium product or commissioned offer live.
- Support and sponsor-intent events measurable.

90-day target:

- 25-100 monthly patrons.
- 1-2 active sponsor conversations.
- 1 commissioned intelligence proposal or paid brief.
- One institutional/grant packet drafted.
- Cross-site traffic and support paths visible in reporting.
